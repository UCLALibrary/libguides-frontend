Bartlett, Paul, "15 Best Things To Do in Almaty", 2023; Condé Nast Traveller.

Welcome to the UCLA Library's research guide for Central Asia. This guide serves as a comprehensive portal to electronic resources related to this historically rich and diverse region, often associated with the ancient Silk Road. Focusing on Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan, Uzbekistan, and Afghanistan, this guide connects you to a wide array of scholarly e-resources. Explore online dictionaries, encyclopedias, bibliographies, databases, indexes, e-book and journal collections, as well as online archives, image repositories, and maps. Please note that while some resources are freely available, others are subscription services provided by the UCLA Library, so remote access may be necessary when accessing from off-campus.
