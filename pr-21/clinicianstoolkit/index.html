  • Skin of Color Image Atlas This link opens in a new window
    “The Journal of the American Academy of Dermatology Skin of Color Image Atlas is a compilation of images derived from published articles in JAAD, JAAD Case Reports, and JAAD International that is meant be a useful resource for the dermatology community.”
  • Skin Deep This link opens in a new window
    “The aim is to develop a free, open-access bank of high-quality photographs of medical conditions in a range of skin tones for use by both healthcare professionals and the public. The team behind Skin Deep hopes that with time, this will improve education and recognition of conditions in all skin tones, reducing inequalities and improving patient care by ensuring that all children receive a timely diagnosis regardless of their skin colour.”

Literature Reviews

The Biomedical Library offers assistance publishing literature reviews - whether they are stand-alone reviews or part of a larger piece of original research. Use this form for the Health Sciences Expert Searching portal and select "Literature Review."

If you're looking for places to publish your review (or other publications), you might try the Journal Author Name Estimator (JANE). JANE will make suggestions based on the text you enter, and identifies open access publishers.

Publishing Case Reports

There isn't an easy way to determine whether or not a journal will publish case reports. The best method is to consult with colleagues and use their professional expertise, or consult the Journal Author Name Estimator (JANE) to find journals that map to the content of your article. Then look at each journal's submission policies. This is often found under areas called some version of "Information For Authors," "Editorial Policies," or "Submission Requirements." 

You can also look for guidance at a list from a 2023 excel file posted on the Open Science Framework. That file comes from the work described in the following publication:

Gotschall, T., Spencer, A., Hoogland, M. A., Cortez, E., & Irish, E. (2023). Journals accepting case reports. Journal of the Medical Library Association : JMLA, 111(4), 819–822.

Open Access Support

A quick way to view a specific journal's agreements with UC is to search for the title at the University of California's Journal Open Access Lookup Tool. 

You can look for open access publishing policies on a broader set of titles (not specific to UC or UCLA) by using Jisc Policy Finder. Jisc Policy Finder can also help you find open access policies from funding agencies (NIH, NSF, etc.). 

  • Open Access Publishing Agreements and Discounts This link opens in a new window
    The University of California is committed to making it easier and more affordable for UC authors to publish open access.

    The UC libraries negotiate agreements with scholarly journal publishers to reduce the amount authors must pay when they choose to publish open access. Most agreements offer up to full coverage of the open access fees; others offer a discount.

    Find details on the amount of funding available to UC authors, by publisher, through the above link. Both full coverage and discount agreements are listed.
