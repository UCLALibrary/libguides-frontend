UCLA Chinese Studies Resources (in Chinese Language)

This guide is intended for faculty, students and researchers with a focus on Chinese studies, offering information on UCLA online resources in Chinese language.  The guide provides pathways to a variety of resource formats: online electronic books, journals and magazines, news sources, and government documents, as well as film and video, sound-recordings, and statistical data sets.

Please note that off-campus access to licensed resources requires user authentication through the proxy server or VPN.

Chinese Studies Purchased/Subscribed Databases

  • Central Daily News, 1928-2006 中央日報, 1928-2006 This link opens in a new window
    This database is back files of Central Daily News, the official newspaper of the Nationalist Party of China (KMT), one of the major parties in Taiwan. This is an important research resource for China/Taiwan studies.
  • CHANT (Chinese Ancient Texts) Database 香港中文大學古文獻資料庫 (華夏文庫) This link opens in a new window
    The database of Chinese ancient texts covers a wide range of early historical periods, including oracle bone scripts, bamboo slips and silk manuscripts, bronze inscriptions, as well as historical literature from Pre-Qin and Han era to Wei, Jin and Southern and Northern Dynasties. This full-text searchable database is particularly focusing on pre-600 AD traditional and excavated Chinese texts.
  • Taiwan AIRITI Library (including TEPS) 華藝線上圖書館 (含臺灣電子期刊服務網) This link opens in a new window
    Online database from Taiwan that includes electronical books and TEPS (Taiwan Electronic Periodical Services), the largest online database that provides journal articles. iRead eBooks (華藝中文電子書) include full-text electronic publications from Taiwan. UCLA participates a UC systemwide patron driven acquisition project with AIRITI, so users may select titles directly on UC Search.
  • BUDA-The Buddhist Digital Archives 佛教数字档案馆 This link opens in a new window
    Via the database, users may access almost any text from the major Buddhist traditions. BUDA contains 30 million pages scanned from the precious originals, and 5 million pages of e-texts.
  • China Contemporary Political Campaigns / 中国当代政治运动数据库 This link opens in a new window
    The database include sub-databases on Chinese Cultural Revolution, Chinese Anti-Rightists Campaign, Chinese Great Leap-Forward & Great Famine, and Chinese Political Campaigns in the 1950s: from Land Reform to the State-Private Partnership.
  • China Ebook Hub 中国电子书库（eReading 易阅通） This link opens in a new window
    The eReading Platform brings you all-new information search and reading experiences and helps you do your professional work better. The Chinese interface of the eReading Platform is easy to use. The quick and convenient search function of the Chinese Library Classification can help you to get the electronic resource you need in the shortest time. The personalized reading tool provides user-friendly guidance. Its recommendation function builds the channel to communicate with the librarians so that your needs can be known very quickly.
  • China Economy, Public Policy, and Security Database 中国当代政治经济皮书 This link opens in a new window
    Pishu, which literally means “cover-books,” are high-value analytical reports intended to guide policymaking in the People’s Republic of China. Pishu are published by the prestigious Social Sciences Academic Press, a branch of the Chinese Academy of Social Sciences. These reports feature exclusive research and analysis by the Chinese Academy of Social Sciences and authors from similar institutions, and present objective analysis versus official government messaging on strategic issues. In China, Pishu are consumed by think tanks and top policymakers. Covered topics include a variety of issues of importance to China's government and research community, including international relations, agricultural policy, and industry competitiveness. They may be considered somewhat analogous to United States Congressional Research Service reports and analysis reports published by the RAND Corporation.
  • Chinamaxx Digital Library of Chinese E-books 超星中文集献 This link opens in a new window
    Chinamaxx Digital Library, powered by Superstar, is one of the largest digital libraries for Chinese electronic books. Superstar provides two methods to find out the books. You can input the title of the book, the author, ISBN, and so directly in the search bar. Or, you may browse the subject categories for books related your research or interest of reading.
  • CNBKSY 民国时期期刊全文数据库、全国报刊索引 This link opens in a new window
    CNBKSY provides the one-stop services of more than 50,000 titles of newspapers and periodicals and over 50 million pieces of literature, with an annual update of more than 5 million entries. Such rare digital resources as the Late Qing Dynasty Periodical Full-text Database (1833～1911), Chinese Periodical Full-text Database (1911～1949) and The North-China Daily News & Herald Newspapers and Hong Lists (1850～1951) have been launched one after another.   • Dacheng Data 大成老旧刊全文数据库 This link opens in a new window
    Dacheng Data collects journal articles from the late Qing period, as the starting time of Chinese journals, down to 1949. The database includes over 180 million articles from 13 million issues of over 7,000 journals published in China during the period. It provides comprehensive and unique research resources to humanities, social sciences and other academic fields.
  • Diaolong Database of Chinese and Japanese Ancient Books 雕龙-中日古籍全文检索数据库 This link opens in a new window
    This database for ancient Chinese and Japanese texts is a massive database for the full-text retrieval of ancient Chinese and Japanese works, developed by a team of experts from mainland China, Japan, and Taiwan. The Database offers two distinct reading interfaces, original Image and full text, facilitate data verification and cross-referencing. In addition to providing detailed information on editions, sources, and compilation history, the Gazetteer section explicitly maps the correspondence between historical and contemporary administrative place names.
  • DragonSource Periodicals 龙源期刊网 This link opens in a new window
    A searchable database of electronic journals/magazines in the original printed looking, particularly Chinese general reading magazines, with full color images and searchable function. Its full-color images provide significant added value especially for the titles in archeology, art and films. The database makes the newest issue available to the users, almost at the same time as the most current printed issue coming to market.
  • Du Xiu 读秀学术搜索 This link opens in a new window
    Provided by Super Star, this powerful database for searching Chinese publications published in mainland China, viewable up to 17 pages of each title. Document delivery for the electronic books listed in the database.
  • EPS China Data 中国数据平台 This link opens in a new window
    The platform includes over 100 databases of China statistical and census data, covering various subjects, industries, fields and all regions of China. The platform contains over 200,000 basic indicators and over 1.2 million combined indicators in time series with annual increment of over 1.1 billion numeric data.
  • Erudition Chinese Classic Ancient Books and Local Gazetteers 爱如生典海: 中國基本古籍庫、中國方志庫合集 This link opens in a new window
    UCLA subscribes to the Basic Chinese Ancient Books database features important classics from the pre-Qin period to the Republic of China era in 4 sub-databases, 20 major categories, and 100 subcategories, a total data size of 330 GB. The Chinese Local Chronicles Library (First and second collection) includes thousands of provincial, prefectural, and county gazetteers from various dynasties. Each one has been digitally reproduced based on a rare edition, preserving all the information of the original book, with each page compared to an image of the original. The subscription also includes the digital Siku Quanshu and Sibu Congkan. Please access Sibu Congkan 四部叢刊 via 數字叢書.
  • Hong Kong and Macau Index to Chinese Periodicals 港澳中文期刊论文索引 This link opens in a new window
    An online index to over 150 periodicals published in Hong Kong and Macao since 1990, with some titles as early as 1950s. It includes only the articles related to social, economic, political, and current events, as well as social sciences.
  • Modern Newspaper in China, 1840-1949 近代报纸数据库 This link opens in a new window
    Published by the National Library of China (NLC), the full-text searchable database includes 100 historical Chinese newspapers published in 1840-1949.
  • People's Daily (1946- ) 人民日报电子版 This link opens in a new window
    With a current circulation of 3 million, People's Daily is the most influential and authoritative newspaper in China. Created in May 1946, People's Daily carries the latest news dispatches of policy information and resolutions of the Chinese Government and major domestic news and international news releases from China. UCLA has acquired access to its complete full-text content of 1946-2005. A separate database covering 1995-present is freely available to the public. It is searchable by date, author, title, column, page, section, and keyword. It also can be browsed by date. All articles can be displayed and printed in full text.
  • Scripta Sinica 漢籍全文資料庫 This link opens in a new window
    This academia resource is compiled by the Institute of History and Philology (IHP) of Academia Sinica (中研院歷史語言研究所) in Taiwan. Began in 1984, Scripta Sinica is the largest Chinese full text database to encompass an enormous breadth of historical materials of this scale, with more than 1,540 titles and 849,689,381. The database contains almost all of the important Chinese classics, especially those related to Chinese history.
  • Shen Bao, 1872-1949 申報, 1872-1949 This link opens in a new window
    Shen Bao was the most important Chinese newspaper in late Qing and Republican China, started on April 30, 1872, and ceased on May 27, 1949 with a total of 25,600 issues. This Shanghai based newspaper was the most influential newspaper in pre-revolutionary China for 78 years, comprehensively covering all the aspects in politics, economy, culture, society, literature and academic studies. With the full-text searchable function, the database comes with added value to the scholars in the fields of modern Chinese history, modern Chinese literature and culture, as well as sociology, anthropology and geographic/demographic studies.
  • Taiwan Academic Classics 台灣學術經典 This link opens in a new window
    Taiwan Academic Classics (TAC) features exclusive and core journals from leading institutions such as Academia Sinica and the National Palace Museum, alongside rare archives. TAC presents 4.5 billion characters, 475+ journals, 370,000+ articles, and 35,000+ issues of journals and books, all fully searchable. UCLA acquired the following databases.
    Biaodian Gujin Tushu Jicheng標點古今圖書集成
    Taiwan Wenxian Congkan臺灣文獻叢刊
    National Palace Museum Journals Archive 故宮期刊知識庫
    Sinica Sinoweb 中研院經典人文學刊
  • UDNDATA 全文報紙資料庫 This link opens in a new window
    UDNDATA is a newspaper database of the United Daily News Group, the largest news group in Taiwan. UDNDATA covers seven Chinese newspapers in Taiwan, Europe and North America: Lian He Bao (聯合報), Jing Ji Ri Bao (經濟日報), Min Sheng Bao (民生報), Lian He Wan Bao (聯合晚報), Xing Bao (星報) in Taiwan, Ouzhou Ri Bao (歐洲日報) in Europe and Meizhou Shi Jie Ri Bao (美洲世界日報) in North America. This online database provides current and searchable full-text articles, as well as searchable images, published on the above seven newspapers. It is not only a major information source on Taiwan, but also an important information source on overseas Chinese in Europe and North America.
  • Wanfang Data 万方数据 This link opens in a new window
    An affiliate of Chinese Ministry of Science and Technology and the leading information provider in China since the 1950s. UCLA subscribes to the following databases focusing on humanities and social sciences.

  • WISENEWS 慧科新聞 This link opens in a new window
    WiseNews is a newspaper database based in Taiwan, which provides hundreds of newspapers published in Taiwan, Hong Kong, mainland China and some other parts of the world.
  • Zhonghua Ancient Books Database 中华经典古籍库 This link opens in a new window
    This authoritative edition of the Chinese classics includes most commonly cited Chinese classics in traditional categories of Jing (Confucian Classics), Shi (History), Zi (Philosophy)and Ji (Literature), consisting 10,900 titles from various historical periods with a total of 3 billion characters so far.
  • China Studies Discovery 中国研究发现 This link opens in a new window
    This China Studies Knowledge Discovery System integrates over 220,000 bibliographic and academic records, utilizing knowledge graphs, semantic analysis, and visualization technologies to search for books, articles, conference proceedings, etc.
  • Chinese Big Set Table of Contents 中国大套图书目次库 This link opens in a new window
    The Database, developed by China National Publications Import & Export (Group) Corporation (CNPIEC), primarily collects table of contents information from large-scale sets of Chinese books. It is a highly practical online resource retrieval and discovery tool for the academic community. Its supports full-text search and discovery, with both simplified and traditional Chinese characters.
  • The Chinese Soviet Republic 中华苏维埃共和国档案 This link opens in a new window
    The Chinese Soviet Republic database is made up of almost 2,500 records spanning 1922 to 1956. The collection is especially concentrated between the establishment of the Chinese Soviet Republic (CSR) in November 1931 and its effective collapse in 1934 under pressure from the Kuomintang (KMT) Army’s encirclement campaign. The collection contains constitutions, directives, and resolutions that formed the basis of the CSR government. Included as well are denunciations, military reports, and court decisions that reflect the struggle to maintain the CSR through the war.
  • CNKI: China National Knowledge Infrastructure 中国知网 This link opens in a new window
    CNKI provides one-stop digital resources including academic journals, dissertations, e-books, newspapers, and conference papers to individuals and more than 1,000 institutions in over 100 countries and regions outside China, delivering high-quality and reliable knowledge services. UCLA subscribes to the following databases as listed. For its full-text articles from Chinese journals, the subscription includes series F (literature / history / philosophy), G (politics / military affairs / law), H (education & social sciences), and J (economy & management).

  • The Eastern Miscellany 東方雜誌 This link opens in a new window
    The Eastern Miscellany is one of the major journals published in pre-1949 China. it is an essential resource for study the period.
  • History of the Communist Party of China 中共党史经典文献数据库 This link opens in a new window
    The database features a core collection of historical archives of the Communist Party of China, including documents from various Party Congresses, writings, autobiographies, chronologies, and manuscripts of party leaders, primary records of major party and state decisions and events from the 12th through the 18th Party Congresses, and selected primary materials dating from the Third Plenary Session of the 11th Central Committee onwards. The complete database encompasses over 200 volumes with approximately 180 million characters.
  • Jiefangjun Bao (PLA Daily) 解放军报 This link opens in a new window
    The People's Liberation Army Daily, 解放军报, (PLA Daily) published since 1956, stands as a rich repository of military news and related information emanating from China's authoritative military establishment. As the official newspaper of the Central Military Commission, the PLA Daily not only chronicles the evolving narrative of China's military affairs but also extends into broader policy realms, mirroring and influencing public discourse in alignment with the Chinese Communist Party's ideological framework. Owned and operated by the Political Work Department of the Central Military Commission, PLA Daily, with its vast collection of editorials, news stories, and military communications, presents a robust academic and analytical platform for scholars, policy analysts, and anyone keen on exploring the military narrative of China from the heart of its official military media outlet.
  • Library Science Literature of the Republican Period 民国图书馆学文献数据库 This link opens in a new window
    Complied by the National Library of China (NLC), this full-text searchable database collects 283 book titles and 13,187 articles published in 1911-1949, specifically on library science in Republican China.
  • The National History Database of P.R.C. 中华人民共和国国史数据库 This link opens in a new window
    The database consists of 241 volumes containing over 100,000 illustrations. The database carries authoritative materials on the economic, social, cultural and political developments since the founding of the People's Republic of China.
  • Renmin University of China Digitized Newspapers and Periodicals 人大复印报刊资料库 This link opens in a new window
    Originally published in a facsimile form, this database for selected full-text Chinese newspaper and journal articles is a useful alternative to other Chinese academic journals.
  • Selected Late Qing and Republican-Era Chinese Newspapers 晚清民国中文报纸 This link opens in a new window
    The Late Qing and Republican-Era Chinese Newspapers collection provides invaluable perspective on this critical period. The press of more than twenty cities is represented, spanning the Chinese mainland and the entire half century. The collection provides researchers a richly comprehensive perspective on Chinese life, culture, and politics throughout the collapse of the Qing Dynasty, the years of provisional government and civil war, and the birth of the People’s Republic.
  • Taiwan Electronic Periodical Service(TEPS) 臺灣電子期刊服務網 This link opens in a new window
    Mainly includes academic journals in Chinese and foreign languages published since the 1990s in Taiwan, mainland China, Hong Kong, Macau, the United States, Singapore, Malaysia, Japan, and Korea. Article topics cover disciplines such as Humanities, Basic &Applied Sciences, Health& Medical Care, Bio-Agriculture, Engineering, and Social Sciences.
